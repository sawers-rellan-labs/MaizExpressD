## Methods

### Data
3′ mRNA-seq of the maize 282 diversity panel from Kremling et al. 2018 (Nature 555:520; SRA PRJNA383416): 1,960 single-end libraries of about 300 inbred lines in seven tissues at four stages: germinating root and shoot; the base, middle (26 lines) and tip of seedling leaf 3; mature adult leaf after flowering, sampled at mid-day and at mid-night; and kernels 350 growing degree days after pollination. Adult leaf was sampled in two groups of lines on different dates (marked 8 and 26 in the library names), so day and night are compared within line and group. A small pilot set includes mid-leaf libraries (shown as “Leaf 3 middle (pilot)”, display only). Tissue definitions follow Lei et al. 2025 (G3 15:jkaf140) and Giri et al. 2021. Lines are named as in the library names; their subpopulations, origins and pedigrees come from Lei et al. 2025 Table S1 (the 12 MR landraces are not in Lei's set). Every library was requantified here; Lei et al.'s results are shown alongside.

### Quantification
Reads were downloaded on NC State's Hazel cluster and processed with nf-core/rnaseq 3.27.0: fastp trimmed the poly(A) tail and everything after it, poly-G and poly-X runs, and reads shorter than 20 nt; salmon (selective alignment, decoy-aware index) quantified them against B73 RefGen_v5 gene models Zm00001eb.1 without length correction, as suits 3′ tags (one tag per transcript whatever its length). Expression is shown as CPM: reads on the gene per million reads on genes. Salmon's TPM is not used: it divides by transcript length, which 3′ tags do not need. Other lines are mapped to B73.

### Library QC (strict)
A library is left out if it has fewer than 1 M mapped reads, maps more than 10 points below what its tissue, flowcell and line predict, has a different library type from the rest, disagrees with the other libraries of B73 in its tissue (or with its repeat library) while they agree with each other, carries tissue from another organ (root, shoot, leaf or kernel marker genes at several times their usual share), looks like another tissue than its label (or, for adult leaf, like the other time of day), or looks like another line than its label across its tissues. One line's adult-leaf day and night labels were found swapped by expression and corrected. A line's repeat libraries in a tissue are averaged.

### Expression per line and tissue
CPM per line and tissue is the mean of the line's libraries; B73 is the mean of its replicate libraries (6 to 25 per tissue). Per tissue, the median and quartiles are taken over lines, with B73 counted once. The pilot leaf-3 base libraries are not used.

### Tissue contrasts
Twelve contrasts compare tissues within lines. **Panel** contrasts pair each line with itself, so genetic differences between lines do not enter the tissue effect: edgeR quasi-likelihood (`~ line + tissue`, TMM normalisation, robust QL dispersion), with B73 left out; night vs day is paired by line and sampling group, and also fitted for each group alone. **B73** contrasts use B73's replicate libraries (DESeq2, `~ tissue`). log2FC = log2(first tissue / second tissue). Genes need at least 10 reads in 10% of the contrast's libraries. Reported: unshrunken log2FC and SE, BH-adjusted p within the contrast, and ashr-shrunken log2FC for display. A fold change carried by one library (one library holds over 90% of the gene's normalised counts, or the difference disappears without the top library) gets no p-value. With 180 or more line pairs most expressed genes reach padj < 0.05, so the effect size matters more than the p-value; the DEG counts use a 2-fold threshold. Most line pairs were sequenced on different flowcells, which cannot be modelled; B73's replicates show flowcell effects are small. Adult and seedling leaf differ in stage, sampling date and possibly growth conditions.

### Tissue specificity
**tau** (Yanai et al. 2005) on log2(median CPM + 1) of the seven main tissues; a gene with tau ≥ 0.8 is called specific to its highest tissue (not computed when no tissue median reaches 1 CPM). **Lei's method**, as in Lei et al.'s code (after Finucane et al. 2018): among genes detected in more than half of the lines of every tissue, log(CPM + 0.25) of every line and tissue is regressed on a tissue indicator (+1 for the tested tissue, −1 for the rest); a gene is specific when its t statistic is in that tissue's top 5% and its median is more than 2-fold every other tissue's. So Lei's method finds tissue-enriched genes that are expressed everywhere, while tau finds genes restricted to a tissue. Lei et al.'s published lists (B73 v3, their quantification) are shown on v5 genes; for genes with a one-to-one match, 96–100% have their highest median here in the same tissue.

### Variation among lines
Per tissue, for genes with median CPM ≥ 5: the variance of log2(CPM + 1) among lines, one library per line (the deepest when a line has several), and the share of it above the variance among B73's replicate libraries in that tissue, which measures plant, batch and read-depth noise within one line. The share is not a heritability: lines also differ in sampling date (adult leaf) and flowcell. Genes whose B73 replicates disagree on and off (variance of log2 CPM above 4, pooled within sampling group) are flagged on the gene card; most are nuclear or scaffold copies of chloroplast or mitochondrial genes that collect organelle reads unevenly, so their CPM is not reliable.

### Co-expression
Within each tissue, the Pearson correlation of log2(CPM + 1) across lines, one value per line (zeros counted). Adult leaf is centred within its two sampling dates first; “within flowcell” also removes each flowcell's mean (partial correlation given flowcell), which removes sequencing batch effects but also some genetic differences between groups of lines sequenced together. Fisher z with SE 1/√(df − 1), df = lines − levels − 1. The seven main tissues are pooled with DerSimonian–Laird random effects; L3Mid (26 lines) and the pilot leaf-3 middle are shown but not pooled. **Strongest partners** are precomputed for every gene with mean CPM ≥ 1: the top 25 by r in each tissue, and by pooled r over the tissues that express both genes (a list needs at least 4 of the 7, and so does a pair). Genes flagged as unstable in B73 are left out of that tissue. Near-identical paralogs share reads and can co-vary for that reason alone; partners within 100 kb are marked “nearby”.

### Lei et al. 2025 results
Lei et al. reanalysed the same data on B73 v3 gene models (1,771 libraries, 298 lines): tissue-specific genes, eQTLs in six tissues (kernel had too few), transcriptome-wide association (TWAS) with agronomic traits, QTLs from published studies colocalized with eQTLs (within 500 kb and within 100 bp), a check of gene–trait correlations in the CUBIC population, and a short list of candidate genes. Their v3 genes are matched to v5 through MaizeGDB's B73 v5–v3 cross-reference. Split or merged gene models and tandem clusters make some matches many-to-many; every match is kept and labelled (1:1, 1:n, n:1, n:m), and results of a v3 gene appear on each of its v5 genes. 96% of Lei's genes have a v5 match. eQTL and QTL positions are Lei's own v3 and v5 coordinates (a few have no v5 position).

Trait codes: DTS and DTA, days to silking and to anthesis (GDD: in growing degree days); ASI, anthesis–silking interval; PH, plant height; EH, ear height; PE, PH minus EH; EP, EH divided by PH; PD, PH divided by days to anthesis; other traits are named in full.

### Gene annotation
MaizeGDB Zm00001eb.1 symbols, names, gene products and GO terms; v3/v4 cross-references from MaizeGDB; Arabidopsis orthologs and gene features from Ensembl Plants. Descriptions marked “predicted” come from MaizeGDB's EnTAP annotation. From MaizeMine (MaizeGDB's data warehouse): metabolic pathways, GWAS hits within 10 kb (GWAS Atlas, NAM GWAS), protein-changing variants (Ensembl Plants, with SIFT), the gene's pan-gene group across B73 and the NAM founders, and the B73 expression atlas (mean FPKM per tissue and stage, full-length RNA-seq under standard conditions). A GWAS hit near a gene is not evidence that the gene causes the trait. **Literature** counts gene-specific papers from NCBI gene2pubmed (papers linked to at most 10 maize genes), a lower bound.

### Caveats
- Lines are mapped to B73, which biases expression of genes whose sequence differs from B73 and misses genes that B73 lacks.
- 3′ tags measure the transcript end only; genes with alternative 3′ ends or near-identical paralogs can share or split reads.
- Libraries of one tissue span several flowcells, and adult leaf spans two sampling dates; both shape variation among lines.
- Lei et al.'s results come from a different quantification on v3 gene models; a v3 gene that matches several v5 genes is shown on each.
